PI Extraction Guidelines
-------------------------

Purpose:
This document defines the default extraction style and workflow for processing award agreements
into meeting-ready PI Checklists. It ensures consistency across all project chats.

Default Extraction Style:
- Output is structured to match the PI Checklist form order exactly.
- Information is presented in plain language first, with citations provided at the end of each item.
- The extraction is designed to be digestible for the PI and Post Award team, serving as the live meeting document.

Source Tags:
Each detail is labeled with a source tag to indicate its origin:
- [Doc] – Directly stated in the award agreement.
- [Interp] – Inferred from multiple parts of the agreement.
- [Absent] – Not in the agreement; value derived from omission.
- [Internal] – Filled from CSUCI records or post-award sources.

Follow-Up Icons:
Icons are used to highlight follow-up needs:
- 🔷 Information typically sourced from other internal docs or finalized after the meeting.
- 🟡 Mentioned but unclear/incomplete; confirm with sponsor or PI.
- 🟥 Missing & time-sensitive; resolve before or during the setup meeting.

Command Trigger:
Use the shorthand phrase: "Nova, run the PI extraction."
This triggers a full extraction in the default style. Alternate phrasing like "PI extraction" will also be recognized.

Workflow Notes:
- Extractions are meeting-ready and can be sent directly to the PI and Post Award team.
- Plain language is prioritized for clarity; citations are included for quick verification.
- A Follow-Up section at the bottom isolates 🟡 and 🟥 items as discussion points.
- The Common Clauses Library will be updated behind the scenes with clause text and locations for future reference.

Example Extraction Snippet
----------------------------------

Award Extraction
- Sponsor: Example Sponsor [Doc] (p. 1)
- Date Received: 09/25/2025 [Internal]

Investigator(s)
- Investigator Name(s): Dr. Jane Doe [Doc]
- Program: Biology [Doc]
- Email: [Internal]
- Extension: [Internal]

Division & Department
- Division: [Internal]
- Department: Biology [Doc]
- Department Number: [Internal]
- Department Chair / Supervisor: [Internal]

Award Information
- Sponsoring Agency: Example Sponsor [Doc]
- Program Name: Research Excellence Program [Doc]
- Title of Proposal: Study of Coastal Ecosystems [Interp]
- Award Number: [🟡 Absent] – Not provided in agreement.
- Proposal Number: [Internal]
- Q Number: [Internal]
- NSF HERD Field: [Internal]
- Award Category: [Internal]

Project Period
- Start Date: 07/01/2025 [Doc] (p. 2)
- End Date: 06/30/2028 [Doc] (p. 2)

Budget
- Award Amount: $100,000 [Doc] (p. 3)
- Match Amount: $0 [Absent]
- Total Budget: $100,000 [Doc] (p. 3)

Grant Provisions
- Termination [Doc]: Sponsor may terminate with 30 days’ notice; PI may terminate for nonpayment.
- Amendments [Doc]: Changes must be in writing and signed by both parties.
- Carry Forward [Absent]: Not addressed in agreement.
- Name Use [Doc]: Sponsor’s name/logo cannot be used without written permission.
- Intellectual Property [Doc]: Sponsor retains ownership of all work products.

Reporting Requirements
Programmatic Reporting (PI Responsibility)
- Type of Reports [Interp]: Annual progress updates and a final report.
- Due Dates [Absent]: No specific dates provided.
- Submission Method [Doc]: Reports submitted to Sponsor’s representative.

Financial Reporting (Grant Accountant Responsibility)
- Invoicing Instructions [Doc]: Quarterly invoices required.
- Report Types [Absent]: No additional financial reports beyond invoices.
- Special Notes [Doc]: Payments capped at $100,000 total.

Follow-Up (Meeting Discussion Points)
- 🟡 Award Number – Confirm with sponsor records.
- 🔷 PI Email – Add from CSUCI directory.
- 🔷 NSF HERD Field – Add from internal classification.
